Your Visit

Your filler appointment, step by step

A calm, transparent, and personalized clinical journey from start to finish.

1

Share your goals

Discuss the features you would like to enhance, any previous filler and your medical history.

2

Review your personalized plan

Your provider assesses your face and explains suitable products, proposed treatment areas, estimated cost and potential risks.

3

Proceed when you feel informed

If treatment is appropriate and you choose to proceed, filler is placed in the planned areas. Comfort measures are discussed as part of your appointment.

4

Allow time for swelling to settle

Some volume changes may be visible immediately, but early swelling can affect your appearance. Follow aftercare instructions and allow time before assessing your settled result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Filler questions, answered

Clear, honest answers to help you make well-informed decisions about your care.

Your consultation includes a discussion of the degree of change you want. Our focus is proportion and an individualized plan. Results depend on your anatomy, the product and amount used, and your response to treatment.
The amount depends on the treatment area, your existing volume and your goals. Your provider will recommend an amount after assessing your face; a larger amount is not automatically the right choice.
Many filler results last approximately six to eighteen months, although longevity depends on the product, treatment area and individual factors. Your provider will explain the expected duration for your specific plan.
Swelling, bruising, tenderness and redness can occur. Recovery varies, particularly around the lips. Discuss any upcoming events before scheduling and follow the aftercare advice provided.
Some hyaluronic acid fillers can be reduced using hyaluronidase when clinically appropriate. Dissolving is a medical treatment with its own risks and limitations; it is not a guaranteed or risk-free reversal. Other filler types cannot be dissolved this way.
Pricing depends on the product, treatment areas and amount of filler recommended. Contact our team for current pricing and consultation fees. Your provider can discuss an estimate based on your individual plan.
Alongside swelling and bruising, possible complications include infection, lumps and allergic reactions. Rare but serious complications from injection into a blood vessel can include tissue damage, blindness or stroke. Your provider will explain the risks, alternatives and symptoms that require immediate medical attention.
